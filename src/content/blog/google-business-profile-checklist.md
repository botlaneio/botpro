When someone searches for a business like yours nearby, your Google Business Profile is often the first thing they see, before your website. It's free, and most of what matters takes minutes. Here's a checklist, in the order worth doing it.

## 1. Get the basics exactly right

- **Name:** your real business name, as it appears on your signboard. Don't add keywords to it.
- **Address or service area:** use an address if customers visit you; a service area if you go to them.
- **Phone number and website:** a number that's answered, and your website's main address.

These details should match everywhere: your website, your invoices, your social profiles.

## 2. Choose your categories carefully

Your **primary category** says what you are, as precisely as possible. "Dental clinic" is better than "Health". Add a few secondary categories only if they're genuinely true.

## 3. Set your hours, including the awkward ones

Add your regular hours, and update them for holidays and closures. Few things lose a customer faster than turning up to a shut door that Google said was open.

## 4. Write a plain description

In a few sentences, say what you do, who for, and where. Write it for a person, not for search engines. Skip prices that change often.

## 5. Add real photos

The outside (so people recognise you), the inside, your team and your work. Real, recent photos beat polished stock images every time.

## 6. Make it easy to get in touch

Check that the call button works and the number is answered. Make sure the link to your website goes to a page with a clear way to enquire, and if customers reach you on WhatsApp, put that number where they'll see it.

## 7. Ask for reviews, and reply to them

The best time to ask is right after a job well done. Make it easy: send a direct link. Reply to every review, good or bad, briefly and politely. People read the replies as much as the reviews.

Never write reviews yourself or pay for them; it breaks Google's rules and can get your profile removed.

## 8. Keep it alive

Once a month, check your hours, add a new photo or two, and reply to any reviews or questions. Ten minutes is enough.

## The next step

Your profile gets people to your door. Your website and how quickly you answer decide what happens next. If both are in place, a search can become a customer the same day.
